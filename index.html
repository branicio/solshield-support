The science

Sunburn time isn’t a guess

minutes = MED ÷ (UV index × 1.5)

MED is the Minimal Erythemal Dose — the UV energy your skin absorbs before it visibly reddens. It differs by Fitzpatrick type (200 J/m² for Type I up to 900 J/m² for Type VI), which is why two people standing in the same sun get very different numbers.

Good to know

Common questions

What does SolShield cost?

SolShield is a subscription app: $9.99 per year or $0.99 per month (USD), each with a 7-day free trial. The subscription unlocks the whole app — real-time UV, forecasts, the exposure timer, notifications, history and the Learn section. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, it converts to a paid subscription automatically.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Through Apple, at any time. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, find SolShield and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period and you keep access until then. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us.

Does SolShield track my location?

No. Your location is used on your device to look up the UV index for where you are, and the coordinates are sent to Apple WeatherKit only to answer that query. There are no accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs and no tracking of any kind. Saved locations, UV history and your settings stay on your device.

Where does the UV data come from?

Apple Weather, through Apple’s WeatherKit framework. Risk levels follow the WHO Global Solar UV Index bands — Low 0–2, Moderate 3–5, High 6–7, Very High 8–10, Extreme 11+.

How is sunburn time calculated?

From the Minimal Erythemal Dose: minutes = MED ÷ (UV index × 1.5). MED is the UV energy your skin absorbs before it visibly reddens, and it differs by Fitzpatrick type — 200 J/m² for Type I up to 900 J/m² for Type VI. For Type II skin at UV 7 that is 250 ÷ 10.5 ≈ 24 minutes. Below UV 3 we show “Low burn risk” instead of a number, following WHO guidance. These are estimates for an average individual, not medical advice.

Which languages does SolShield support?

English, Portuguese and Spanish. Switch at any time in the app’s Settings — no restart needed.
